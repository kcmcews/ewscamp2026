Theme Talk 3

Shining As Stars (3): Holiness and Healing of our Brokenness

Romans 5:1-5 (ESV)

Sermon Outlines

“Therefore, since we have been justified by faith, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ. Through him we have also obtained access by faith into this grace in which we stand, and we rejoice in hope of the glory of God. Not only that, but we rejoice in our sufferings, knowing that suffering produces endurance, and endurance produces character, and character produces hope, and hope does not put us to shame, because God's love has been poured into our hearts through the Holy Spirit who has been given to us.”

— Romans 5:1-5 (ESV)
1. Introduction: Coming to Terms with My Broken Self
  • We must dare to confront ourselves and our own wounds and brokenness.
  • Why? Because very often it is our unhealed wounds that hinder our growth in holiness.
  • Two examples:
    • Friend at Bristol who would not face his own deep insecurity.
    • Student who had to face her childhood pains to find healing.
Notes (Section 1) Saved
2. Causes of Wounds in Our Lives
  • Love deficit in our growing years: Babies in orphanages are known to die without “sensing” love.
  • Sense of rejection: Illegitimate child, unwanted baby, or 2nd Chinese daughter syndrome.
  • Over-controlling (even smothering) mothers: Men with such mothers often face challenges in relationships with women (Monty Barker, Consultant Psychiatrist). Research also links smothering mothers combined with absentee fathers to relational struggles.
  • Growing up with shame: Poverty, childhood sexual abuse, neglect, etc.
  • Deep insecurities: Academic performance, physical beauty, money, social status, etc.
Notes (Section 2) Saved
3. The Path to Healing
  • Some hindrances to healing:
    • Lack of self-acceptance and self-hatred.
    • Failure to forgive others.
    • Failure to receive forgiveness.
  • The Gospel’s Answer Rom 5:1-5: Points us to the love of God and His total acceptance of us as the only adequate answer to our pains and wounds.
Notes (Section 3) Saved
4. Transformed to be Like Christ
  • God is actively seeking to bring healing into our lives.
  • We must be courageous enough to face our brokenness and allow God to enter.
  • This requires us to cooperate fully with God. Those who are willing to let God into their lives will be transformed!
