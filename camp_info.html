Camp Guidelines

Do's

  • Be punctual for all worship sessions, devotionals, and meal times.
  • Wear your camp name tag at all times for security and identification.
  • Maintain clean common spaces and respect quiet hours (10:30 PM - 7:00 AM).
  • Keep your personal belongings and room keys secure at all times.

Don'ts

  • Do not enter rooms assigned to the opposite gender.
  • Do not leave the resort grounds without informing committee members.
  • Avoid wasting food or leaving trash in the seminar halls.
  • No smoking, alcohol, or illicit substances anywhere on the resort premises.
