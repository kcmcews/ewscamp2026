Scripture Passages

1 The LORD reigns; let the peoples tremble! He sits enthroned upon the cherubim; let the earth quake!

2 The LORD is great in Zion; he is exalted over all the peoples.

3 Let them praise your great and awesome name! Holy is he!

4 The King in his might loves justice. You have established equity; you have executed justice and righteousness in Jacob.

5 Exalt the LORD our God; worship at his footstool! Holy is he!

6 Moses and Aaron were among his priests, Samuel also was among those who called upon his name. They called to the LORD, and he answered them.

7 In the pillar of the cloud he spoke to them; they kept his testimonies and the statute that he gave them.

8 O LORD our God, you answered them; you were a forgiving God to them, but an avenger of their wrongdoings.

9 Exalt the LORD our God, and worship at his holy mountain; for the LORD our God is holy!

— Psalm 99:1-9 (ESV)

Devotion Material

Purpose of Meditation

The purpose of meditation is to help us hear God more clearly. Through meditation, we invite the Holy Spirit to work in our hearts—to teach us, cleanse us, comfort us, and even rebuke us.

So, during meditation, we create a space for God to work in our hearts and spirits, allowing Christ to build an inner temple in our hearts.

How to Meditate

First of all, pray and ask God to remove every thought that is not from Him—to quiet our own thoughts and to protect us from thoughts that come from Satan—and to allow the Holy Spirit to guide us.

Use all five of your senses to put yourself into the text. Let the Holy Spirit guide you to see, hear, smell, taste, or even touch what the text describes.

Reminder: Don’t be afraid that you might misunderstand God’s Word. When we open our hearts and allow the Holy Spirit to guide us, He will speak to each one of us.
