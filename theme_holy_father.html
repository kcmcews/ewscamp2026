Theme Talk 1

Shining As Stars (1): Holy Father

Phil 2:15; Lev 19:2; Mat 5:48 (ESV)

Sermon Outlines

“...that you may be blameless and innocent, children of God without blemish in the midst of a crooked and twisted generation, among whom you shine as lights in the world...”

— Philippians 2:15 (ESV)
1. Introduction
  • Theme: Holiness or Holy Living
  • "Shining as Stars" Phil 2:15 helps us understand what holy living means. How did the Early Church shine as stars in the Roman world?
    • Core Moral Differences: Sanctity of Life (banned abortion, child abandonment, gladiator games); Sexual Purity (virginity until marriage, faithfulness); Charity & Care (fed poor, cared for plague victims, buried dead); Peaceful Living.
    • Separation from Roman Culture: No Idol Worship; Honest Work (avoided jobs tied to idols, theatre, magic); Close Community (private homes, shared meals).
  • Holy living is NOT:
    • Just being religious (One can be religious and yet not holy; cf. Prophets Amos 5:21-24).
    • Being moral in a negative or legalistic sense (Not life-denying or Pharisaic).
    • Confused with middle-class respectability / snobbery.
    • Confused with the ability to do signs, wonders, or miracles Mat 7:21-23 (e.g., Samson).

2. In What Sense is God Holy?
  • Root meaning is 'to separate':
    • Applied to God: Separate from creation (infinite from finite, perfect from imperfect). God's moral perfection in contrast to our imperfection.
    • Applied to humanity: Separated from the world and unto God (sanctified). Lev 19:2; Mat 5:48
  • A Trinitarian Definition for Holiness:
    • Father Mt 5:48: We are called to become more like our Father ("be like Daddy").
    • Son 1 Cor 11:1: Jesus is the true Son; we model our lives after Him. Meditate on the character of Christ.
    • Spirit Gal 5:19-23: Filled with the Spirit to display the fruit of the Spirit.
  • The Incomparable God Is 40:12-26:
    • Is there anyone wise and strong enough to teach God how to create the world? Is 40:12-14
    • Kingdoms rise and fall, but God remains Lord of history. Is 40:15-17
    • The gods of this world are not comparable to the God of Israel. Is 40:18-20
    • Kings come and go, but Jesus Christ is the same yesterday, today, and forever. Is 40:21-24
    • If you are still worried, look at the stars. Is 40:25-26

3. The Holy God Who Does Not Tolerate Sin
  • Scripture Context: Ex 32:7-10; Ex 33:12-23
  • God's wrath against a stiff-necked people Ex 32:9-10.
  • Our God is a consuming fire; let us offer acceptable worship with reverence and awe Heb 12:28b-29.
  • Moses' prayer: "Please show me your glory" Ex 33:18-23. Man shall not see His face and live; placed in a cleft of the rock.
