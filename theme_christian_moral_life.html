Shining As Stars (2): The Christian Moral Life

Romans 12:1-2 (ESV)

Sermon Outlines

“I appeal to you therefore, brothers, by the mercies of God, to present your bodies as a living sacrifice, holy and acceptable to God, which is your spiritual worship. Do not be conformed to this world, but be transformed by the renewal of your mind, that by testing you may discern what is the will of God, what is good and acceptable and perfect.”

— Romans 12:1-2 (ESV)
1. Introduction
  • “Living sacrifice … transformed by the renewal of your mind” Rom 12:1-2:
    • “Transformed” — from metamorphoo
    • “Mind” — from nous (mind, understanding, will, judgement, insight)
  • United with Christ in His death and resurrection Rom 6:1-4: We died to sin and walk in newness of life.
  • Sons and daughters of God Rom 8:14-17: Led by the Spirit; received the Spirit of adoption crying “Abba! Father!” Heirs of God and fellow heirs with Christ.
  • What do we think of ethics and Christian morality?
    • “...in the NT religion is grace, and ethics is gratitude” (John Stott).
    • Live as our Father’s child! (Cf. Chinese parents saying: “Don’t shame your family!”).

2. God’s Specific Instructions in the Bible
  • The Ten Commandments Ex 20:2-17; Deut 5:6-21: God’s moral laws for His people.
  • The Sermon on the Mount Mat 5–7:
    • The Beatitudes Mat 5:2-12 (= blessedness / happiness).
    • Jesus shifts the focus from literal law observance to the inner intentions of the heart.
  • The Great Commandment Mark 12:29-31 (cf. Deut 6:5; Lev 19:18): Love God wholeheartedly and love your neighbor as yourself. These two sum up the Law.
  • The New Commandment John 13:34-35: Love one another “as I have loved you.” This is the ultimate standard and witness to the world.

3. Learning from Wesley's Pastoral Practice
  • The General Rules of the Methodist Church: Found in the original Methodist Discipline & updated for Malaysian Methodists.
    • A Methodist Guide for Christian Conduct
    Tap to open supplementary PDF guide
  • The Class Meeting: Compulsory for converts; provided active pastoral support and spiritual accountability (not just a fellowship group).
  • Wesley’s Requirements for all Methodists:
    • Public worship of God & ministry of the Word.
    • The Supper of the Lord.
    • Family and private prayer.
    • Searching the Scriptures.
    • Fasting or abstinence.
